LAGOS LIFE SIMULATOR — 0.1.0 PROTOTYPE

Extract every file to one folder, then run LagosLife.exe. Keep LagosLife.pck
beside the executable. Godot and the Godot editor are not required to play.
Windows x86_64; an OpenGL 3.3 compatible graphics driver is required.

Choose New game on the title screen. Continue resumes your saved slot.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: move          E: interact          Escape: close / pause
I: bag                      J: quests            P: contacts
C: career                   Tab: phone           O: wardrobe
K: save (outside menus)
Controller: left stick/D-pad moves; A interacts/confirms; B goes back/pauses;
Y opens the phone; X opens the bag. Controller mappings are tested synthetically;
physical hardware testing is still needed. Menus support arrows and Enter.

YOUR FIRST DAY
Leave the room through the labelled street door. Greet Tunde to the south-east,
ask about delivery work, and start a job. Collect the parcel beside him and take
it to Bisi east of the provisions kiosk. Hand it over for pay. Buy food from
Amara or Sade, open the bag and eat. Return to your room and sleep. Open the
quest journal to claim your first-day reward. Complete a delivery to unlock
Femi's stock matching shift. Visit the clothing stand for new shirt colours.

Rain, traffic and neighbour encounters can occur outside. The phone shows their
choices and NPCs' scheduled locations. Traffic can delay transport. Character
options, phone tab, bank balance, event cooldowns and NPC state are saved.

SAVE AND SETTINGS
Use K or Pause -> Save game. Sleep, travel and completed jobs can autosave.
Use Pause -> Save and return to title before leaving a session. Closing the
window does not automatically save. Starting fresh keeps the old disk slot until
you next save/autosave. Backup recovery is available for corrupt saves.

Windows save directory: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\NIGAME\saves\
Settings: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\NIGAME\settings.cfg

Settings provides Music, Ambience and SFX volume, 100-140% text size, reduced
motion and fullscreen. The game uses original procedural art and a short original
syncopated music loop. It is a small prototype with fictional characters and
prices, not a finished commercial simulation.
